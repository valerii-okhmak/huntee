# Features for Students

Huntee helps students showcase their skills and gain a chance for employment in leading IT companies.

## Key Features:

- **Test Taking**  
  Assessments consist of various question types: multiple choice, open-ended responses, and practical coding tasks.

- **Personalized Experience**  
  Students can choose the area in which they want to be evaluated, such as *Frontend*, *Backend*, *QA*, or *DevOps*.  
  The system automatically assigns the appropriate tests.

- **Results Overview**  
  After completing the test, students immediately see their score, number of correct answers, and completion time.

- **Interview Invitations**  
  If the result is high, companies may invite the student for an interview directly through the platform.  
  This significantly increases their chances of real employment.

- **Feedback**  
  Some companies provide short feedback on the test submission, helping students identify and improve weak areas.

## How to Use:

1. **Register on the Platform**  
   Create a personal candidate account on Huntee.

2. **Choose a Track**  
   Select your preferred specialization (e.g., Frontend, Backend, QA).

3. **Take the Test**  
   Complete the test tasks in the chosen direction within the allotted time.

4. **Analyze the Results**  
   After finishing the test, review your score, statistics, and any feedback.

5. **Wait for a Response**  
   If your score meets expectations, you may be invited to the next hiring stage — a technical or HR interview with a company.

# Huntee App

- [Українська версія](../README.md)

**Huntee** is a web platform for online candidate testing with built-in integrity control mechanisms. The platform provides IT companies with an effective tool for initial candidate screening, and students — with the opportunity to showcase their skills and receive interview invitations.

## Project Overview

Huntee App is a platform that enables IT companies to conduct online testing of candidates as part of their initial recruitment process. Candidates who successfully pass the test may receive job interview invitations. The app offers a simple interface for both administrators and candidates, ensuring a smooth testing and evaluation process.
